Theme

SelloTheme(ink, darkTheme, reducedMotion) (Theme.kt:16) is the only theme. The ink is the user's choice, a ThemeInk (SelloColors.kt:12): one of the hand-made SelloInks, Cobalto, the first-run default, and Violeta (SelloColors.kt:26), or HueInk(hue), the palette the generator builds for Tuya and Sistema (SelloColors.kt:41); light or dark and reduced motion follow the system unless the caller says otherwise. Wallpaper-derived (dynamic) Material colours are never applied: Sistema takes only the wallpaper's hue, and the generator gives Sello's own shades of it.

Dynamic colours

A generator builds a palette from any hue: hueColors(hue, isDark) (HuePalette.kt), a whole OKLCH degree from 0 to 359. It is Sello's own code, with no colour library: each role keeps the luminance of Cobalto's colour for it, so every contrast ratio stays Cobalto's, and turns its OKLCH hue by as much as the hue is from Cobalto's (267°); chroma drops only where sRGB can't hold it. White roles, semantic, the dark goalBand, the scrim and the fixed investment inks stay. At 267° it gives Cobalto exactly, and at every degree, light and dark, it passes every contrast pair SelloPaletteTest checks (HuePaletteTest). Cobalto and Violeta keep their hand-made palettes. hueName gives the hue's Spanish name (Rosa to Fucsia) and hueMeaning says whether it is red (0–49°) or green (122–195°), the hues gain and loss also use. Tuya uses it (SELLO-202): the person picks a hue in Configuración › Apariencia › Tinta, and SelloTheme(ink = HueInk(hue)) draws the whole app in it; a picker starts from SelloInk.hue, each hand-made ink's own brand hue (Cobalto 267°, Violeta 286°). Sistema uses it too (SELLO-203), on Android 12 and later: hueOf(argb) (HuePalette.kt:96) turns the phone's accent (system_accent1_500) into its whole OKLCH degree, rounded to the nearest, 359.5° and above to 0°, and the app is drawn in HueInk of it, the hue as it is, none moved or blocked. A grey accent (a monochrome style) has no hue: hueOf gives none, Sistema is still offered and chosen, and the app is drawn in Cobalto until the phone has a colour again. Android 11 has no accent at all: Sistema is not offered there and is drawn in Cobalto. PhoneColor (None, Grey or a Hue) tells the three apart.

Every colour a screen or resource draws comes from the theme (SELLO-190): no Color(0x…) or Color.White outside the palette files, copied roles set from the role they copy, a scrim role, and a start-up window that is the desk. A scheme therefore reaches every part if it builds SelloColors itself, through lightPalette / darkPalette, as the generator does; a new Material colorScheme alone would move only the stock Material parts.

What it must still provide

  • The roles Material maps: brand, brandSoft, desk, paper*, ink*, outline, rule, pastBar* and scrim (black, as Material's).
  • The roles with no Material equivalent: the dark bands (band, goalBand, investmentBand, deeper than what they stand for), onBand, semantic.warn, warnSoft and the investment inks.
  • semantic.gain and loss (and their soft and on colours) are meanings: they never follow the ink, Tuya's or the wallpaper's.

What stays fixed

  • The window before the first frame (@color/sello_start_window, Cobalto's desk, light or dark as the phone): it is drawn before any Kotlin runs, so it can't know the ink.
  • The Settings ink swatches (ThemeInk.swatch, the ink's light band): each previews its own ink, not the active one. Tuya's is its hue's band (HueInk.swatch), or before it has a hue, a sweep of every generated band with a +; Sistema's is the band of the phone's hue.
  • The launcher icon; Android 13+ themed icons already follow the wallpaper.

Components read tokens through SelloTheme.colors, .type, .shapes, .spacing, .alpha and .reducedMotion (Theme.kt:40). SelloColors.toMaterialColorScheme() (SelloMaterial.kt:12) fills every Material 3 colour slot from Sello tokens, so stock dialogs, pickers and ripples never show a Material baseline colour; the Material slot each role feeds is named in the tables below.

The status bar's icon colour follows what the shown screen draws under it: StatusBarInk(ink) (StatusBarInk.kt:64), called by SelloScaffold; the last screen to speak wins.

Investment colours

SelloColors.investmentInk(rank) (SelloColors.kt:89): rank 0 is the brand, then five fills that repeat every six, light and dark fills alternating so neighbours in a bar never share one. The number on each swatch is drawn in the colour written on that fill: on a light palette's mid fills it is the palette's ink, on a dark palette's light slate the family's light ink, so Violeta's investments carry Violeta's ink.

Type

One bundled variable font sets the whole interface: Schibsted Grotesk, weights 400 to 900 (SchibstedGrotesk, Type.kt:24; res/font/schibsted_grotesk.ttf). Saira Stencil One is for stamps only, never running text (Type.kt:35). There are no downloadable fonts.

Display, headline and title roles hold figures and turn on tabular figures (tnum); body and label roles hold sentences and do not, because in this font tnum also widens points, commas and colons. 12 sp is the smallest text in the app (Type.kt KDoc). Amounts

Amounts are drawn by MoneyText with its own MoneyStyle sizes: Hero 60 sp, Total 44 sp, Card 32 sp, Title 19 sp, Line 14 sp (MoneyText.kt:54), and by FittedMoneyText, which steps the size down to fit.

The SelloType styles after the stamps (Type.kt:83, read through SelloTheme.type) are the combinations of a role with another size or weight that several places repeat. Each is the Material role with only the named change, so switching to it from a hand-written copy draws the same text.

Weights by role

SelloTheme.type.weight (SelloWeights, Type.kt:115) names the weights for a SpanStyle inside a sentence or a fontWeight on an amount, where a whole style does not fit.

Screens set no weight, size or tracking by hand: they use a role, a SelloType style or a weight token. Values with no token, on purpose, each a private constant named in its own component: sizes used once (9 and 14 sp milestone labels; 13, 18 / 22, 29, 37 and 48 sp on the celebration plate; 20 sp for an investment's return; 21 / 24 sp for the month switcher), the amount keypad's 22 / 26 sp keys and a text button's 15 sp label; the 0.05 em of EntryHeading, the 0.06 em of a movement month line and the 0.08 / 0.14 em of the plate's stencil lines; 0.56.em, the raised currency code inside MoneyText. The 0.6 sp tracking is used twice, on two different roles (the expense stub's weekday, labelSmall at 700, and the plan slip's tag, groupLabel), so each component names its own.

Reduced motion

systemReducedMotion() (ReducedMotion.kt:26) is true when the system's animator duration scale is 0, which is what Android's "Remove animations" sets. It listens for the change, so turning animations off takes effect at once. SelloTheme passes it on as SelloTheme.reducedMotion; when true, every animation cuts to its end state (stamps show at rest, skeleton bars do not pulse, circles show their fill).
