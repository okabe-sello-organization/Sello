Feature

Shell

The frame around every screen: tabs, top bars, the month, the Anotar dock, notices, back and the shared ways money, dates and months are written.

Purpose

The shell lets a person move between Recibo, Resumen, Metas and Inversiones, choose which month they are looking at, start an expense from anywhere, and read what just happened to a correction, all in one frame that every feature reuses.

It is SelloAppRoot (navigation and lifecycle), ShellViewModel (month, picker, open category, pending entry, all kept across process death), ShellScreen (the frame each screen passes its content to) and the shared presentation in presentation/. Feature forms (Anotar, editors, receipts) do not use ShellScreen; they draw their own SelloScaffold and are compared with it in App-wide patterns.

Entry points

  • Launcher: MainActivity shows SelloAppRoot once start-up is ready, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/MainActivity.kt:67. Its start destination is Recibo, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:94.
  • A link sello://anotar?categoria=…&monto=… (not browsable): ShellAction.OpenLink, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/MainActivity.kt:119, parsed by EntryLinks.parse, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/EntryLink.kt:33.
  • A tab: navigation.navigate(route) with saved and restored state, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:86.
  • The Anotar dock: ShellAction.StartEntry, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ShellScreen.kt:264; with no category that can take it the category form opens first, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:167, otherwise the expense form, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:174.
  • The month bar or a title's month switcher: ShellAction.OpenMonthPicker, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ShellScreen.kt:217 and :250.

Screen: Tab frame with the month bar (Recibo, Resumen)

Layout

  1. The band (colors.band) reaches 100dp under the status bar (Blotter.Short) and grows with the bar and ribbon, drawn by SelloScaffold.
  2. MonthBar (feature-local, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ShellBars.kt:61): the month name large, the year smaller at 80% onBand, a chevron; the three are one button. Actions sit at the trailing edge (Recibo: the settings gear).
  3. Over a past month, the past-month ribbon (PastMonthRibbon, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ShellBars.kt:160): a calendar icon, "Estás viendo septiembre" with the month bold, and a paper pill "Volver a octubre".
  4. The content on the desk, with 12dp side margins from the scaffold.
  5. A notice, when there is one, floats over the bottom of the content (it moves nothing).
  6. The dock: one full-width EntryDock "+ Anotar". Resumen has none (owner decision noted at app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:49).
  7. The tab bar, SelloNavigationBar, on paper. In a short or wide window the tabs and the dock's "+" move to SelloNavigationRail; with the keyboard open both bottom controls step aside.

System bars

  • SystemBarsFollowTheme (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/platform/SystemBars.kt:26) is the one owner of the window's bar style: both bars transparent, edge to edge; status-bar icons follow what the shown screen says is under them; navigation-bar icons follow Sello's own light or dark theme, which may differ from the phone's.
  • Screens say what is under the status bar with StatusBarInk; SelloScaffold does it for every frame (white on the band, ink on the desk).
  • The month-picker sheet keeps the band's status icons with statusBarLightIconsUnder; full-screen dialogs set their own with DialogBarIcons (the lograda moment).
