Feature

Resumen

How the selected month compares with the one before, where the income went, month-by-month and weekday spending, and at most one suggestion.

Purpose

Lets a person read the selected month as a whole, with nothing projected: the difference with the month before over the same days, every category ranked by what it took from the income, history by month and by weekday, and a suggestion to adjust a limit that was passed more than once. It records nothing; Resumen has no Anotar dock.

Entry points

  • The Resumen tab: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:81, destination app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:41.
  • Recibo's invitation "Ver cómo cerraste …": picks the closed month in the shared selection, then selects the tab (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:122).
  • The month shown is the shared selection (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/MonthSession.kt:33), the same month Recibo and category detail show; it is changed from the shell's month picker.
  • System Back on Resumen goes to the Recibo tab unless the month picker is open (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:61).

Screen: Resumen

Layout

Inside the shell (tabs, month in the title, no Anotar dock, the correction notice bar when there is one). One scrolling column, slips spaced spacing.lg with an extra spacing.lg above and below (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/resumen/ResumenScreen.kt:80). Where selloWindowLayout says two panes and the width is at least 600 dp, the summary slips and the chart slips are two scrolling columns side by side (ResumenScreen.kt:96).

  1. Older-figures notice, only when stale: an ErrorSlip with the default title and the date the figures run to (ResumenScreen.kt:139).
  2. Hero. Without a comparison: AnswerSlip with no stamp, neutral ink, the question as label, what was spent as the amount and one or two notes (ResumenScreen.kt:473). With a comparison: the feature-local ComparisonSlip — the question, the difference as a FittedMoneyText, "más/menos/lo mismo que en …", an arrow and the rate, a TearLine, the basis ("Del 1 al 3 de cada mes") and both months as bars on one scale (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/resumen/SummaryComponents.kt:53). The current month's bar is brand, the earlier one pastBar.
  3. Income slip (Slip, pinked), headed "¿A dónde fue lo que entró?" (ResumenScreen.kt:158):
    • With income: LeaderLine "Entró en octubre", a SplitBar of spent (solid brand) against the rest (striped), the ranking, a LeaderLine "Gastos", a TotalLine "Ingresos menos gastos" (loss ink when overspent), the overspent note and "Movimientos anotados hasta el …" (ResumenScreen.kt:221).
    • Without income: "Aún no anotas ingresos en …" in titleMedium W800, a note, the ranking and a TotalLine "Total" with what was spent (ResumenScreen.kt:168).
    • The ranking: a basis line, then one feature-local SpendingRankRow per category with spending — CategoryCircle 36 dp full, name over a 6 dp share bar, amount, chevron (ResumenScreen.kt:277, SummaryComponents.kt:156).
    • Under a TearLine, a SlipStub with two StubButtons: "Ingresos" with the count of incomes, and "Fijos" (read as "Pagos e ingresos fijos") with the next fixed payment or income (ResumenScreen.kt:187).
  4. Empty instead of the chart slips: EmptySlip with the Insights icon and "Anotar un gasto" (ResumenScreen.kt:338).
  5. "Mes a mes" slip: heading, the "Un mes normal" row and its basis, the selected column's label and amount (with CarryBreakdownSection or "Límite de ese mes" under it), then a ColumnsChart that scrolls sideways when columns would be narrower than 48 dp, with the month's limit as a dashed reference line, the current month striped and starred, short value labels on the highest and current columns, the "* Mes en curso" note and the insufficient-history line (ResumenScreen.kt:349).
  6. "¿Qué días gastas más?" slip: the selected weekday's average, or the answer "Los sábados" in headlineSmall W800, the basis, and a 120 dp ColumnsChart Monday first (ResumenScreen.kt:543).
  7. Suggestion: SuggestionNote with evidence, advice, an action and a dismiss (ResumenScreen.kt:440).

Controls

  • "Reintentar" on the error or stale slip: ResumenAction.Retry, a new read of the same month that keeps the last good figures meanwhile.
  • A category in the ranking: opens that category's detail as its own screen (ResumenCategoryRoute, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:55); category detail is described on Category.
  • "Ingresos" stub: the income list (IncomeHistoryRoute). "Fijos" stub: Pagos e ingresos fijos (SchedulesRoute).
  • "Anotar un gasto" on the empty slip: the expense entry (ExpenseEntryRoute()).
  • A column of either chart: SelectHistory(id) or SelectWeekday(id).
  • Suggestion action "Ajustar el presupuesto de …": the category's editor on the selected month (CategoryEditRoute, ResumenDestinations.kt:57). Dismiss: DismissSuggestion.

Feature-local parts that look like shared ones

  • ComparisonSlip (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/resumen/SummaryComponents.kt:53) rebuilds AnswerSlip's header (design-system/src/main/java/com/software/sello/designsystem/component/Paper.kt:534): its question is bodyLarge W600 with 6 dp under it instead of AnswerSlip's plain inkSoft label, and it has no stamp slot. Both in resumen; AnswerSlip in recibo-cobalto-light-font1.0.png. Finding: C13.
  • ComparisonBars (SummaryComponents.kt:98) and the bar inside SpendingRankRow (SummaryComponents.kt:175) are single-fill bars drawn with Boxes (14 dp with a 4 dp radius; 6 dp with shapes.control) next to the shared SplitBar (design-system/src/main/java/com/software/sello/designsystem/component/SummaryCharts.kt:43) used one slip below.
  • SpendingRankRow resembles Ajustar's LimitRow (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/ReciboComponents.kt:356) and the shared ScheduleRow: circle, name, amount, chevron. It is 52 dp tall (LimitRow 56, ScheduleRow 60) and is not merged into one spoken button. Both rows visible in resumen and adjust-budget.
  • "Un mes normal" (ResumenScreen.kt:351) is the same figure as category detail's "Un mes normal aquí" (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/CategoryInsightSections.kt:230), drawn as a row here and as label-over-figure there, with two identical plurals.
  • The stale notice uses ErrorSlip, where Recibo and its screens use StaleSlip (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/presentation/read/StaleSlip.kt:23). Findings: M01, C06.
