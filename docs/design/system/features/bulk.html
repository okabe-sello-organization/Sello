Feature

Bulk entry

Type several expenses, or several incomes, on one receipt with one date and save them together.

Purpose

"Anotar varios" is a receipt of up to 30 lines. Each line has an amount typed on the pad and a category (for expenses) or a source (for incomes), with an optional note; the receipt has one date. Saving records every line with an amount, or none. Where it was opened from sets the kind of the whole receipt, for good: the title says "Varios gastos" or "Varios ingresos".

Layout

A plain Column spaced by a hard-coded 8.dp (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/bulk/BulkEntryScreen.kt:135), not EntryLayout: the receipt takes the free height and scrolls its lines inside; the choices, the action and the pad stay put below it.

  1. Top bar (SelloScaffold) with no band: X, "Varios gastos" or "Varios ingresos". The removal notice uses the scaffold's notice slot (:120).
  2. BulkReceipt, at least 160 dp tall (:263): the caption ("Anotar varios" and "3 renglones", or a problem), the date FieldPill as headerEnd, the lines, a tear line, "Otro renglón" and "Total". Expense lines show the category circle, name and note; income lines a green "+" mark, the source (or Otro's name with "Otro" under it) and a plus amount in gain green.
  3. Choices (:298): an EntryHeading "Categoría · renglón 3" (or "Origen · renglón 3"; without the line number for a one-line receipt); a stale notice; the highlighted line's chips in ChipRows, its choice first; for "Otro" an EntryLine "¿De dónde vino?" with "Cambiar"; the note's EntryLine.
  4. Action (:412): "Máximo 12 dígitos" as a hint, or the error, then a full-width SelloButton "+ Anotar 3 gastos" / "+ Anotar 3 ingresos" in gain green, the kind's tone (the check-again button too).
  5. AmountKeypad, compact, typing into the highlighted line (:142).

Controls

  • X and Back: Back; asks before discarding typed lines; the X is off while busy, Back is ignored while busy or pending.
  • Tap a line: Select, the pad, chips and note now apply to it. Swipe it towards the start, its long-press menu or the screen-reader action: Remove (not on the last line). "Deshacer": UndoRemove.
  • "Otro renglón": AddLine, once the last line has an amount and there is room.
  • Date pill (read "Fecha: …"): OpenDatePicker; one date for the whole receipt.
  • Category chip: CategoryPicked; source chip: SourcePicked ("Otro" opens the name composer).
  • "Cambiar" on Otro's name: OpenSourceName. Note line: OpenNote.
  • The button: Submit, or Retry when the outcome is unknown. "Reintentar": Retry (read again).
  • The pad: digits, "000", backspace, long press to clear, on the highlighted line.

  • Carry correction: ConfirmSlip "¿Anotar estos gastos?" with CarryCorrectionContent and "Anotar", busy while saving (BulkEntryScreen.kt:169). Expenses only.
  • Date: Material date picker with "Aceptar", no day after today (BulkEntryScreen.kt:566).
