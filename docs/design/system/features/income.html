Feature

Income

Record or correct one income, and see what came in this month and where it went.

Purpose

"Anotar ingreso" records one income from a source with the pad, in gain green with a plus; opened on a recorded income it becomes "Editar ingreso" to correct or delete it. "Ingresos de octubre" shows the month's income as Resumen counts it, how much of it budgets hold, the month's plan, and each income, which opens its correction.

Entry points

  • Income history: Resumen's income slip (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/resumen/ResumenScreen.kt:147), navigating at app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:54.
  • Anotar ingreso: the history's dock "Anotar ingreso" (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/IncomeDestinations.kt:57) and its empty slip (IncomeDestinations.kt:68); "+" on the incomes tab of Pagos e ingresos fijos, opening with Se repite (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:55); the "Ingreso" side of the switch on Anotar (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/expense/ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:242, hosted by EntryDestination at app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ExpenseDestinations.kt:104).
  • Editar ingreso: a row of the history (IncomeDestinations.kt:72) and the catch-up list (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:69).
  • From the history, "Anotar varios" opens Varios ingresos (IncomeDestinations.kt:74).

Screen: Anotar ingreso / Editar ingreso

Layout

The same EntryLayout and brand band as Anotar (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/income/IncomeFormScreen.kt:174); the comment there keeps the expense band because green is the goals'. Green is carried by the amount and the button.

  1. Top bar (SelloScaffold) on the band: X, "Anotar ingreso" or "Editar ingreso", and "Borrar" while correcting (:147).
  2. Header: SegmentedSwitch "Gasto | Ingreso" at index 1, for a new income opened from the expense form's host (:178).
  3. EntryAmountSlip "Ingreso en pesos" in ButtonTone.Gain: a bank icon, the day pill, the amount drawn with a plus (:260).
  4. MoreSlip "Otro ingreso" (:194).
  5. Choices (:295): stale notice; source chips, words only, chosen first, in ChipRows; for "Otro" an EntryLine "¿De dónde vino?" with "Cambiar"; the note's EntryLine; Se repite's row (new income only); a standing bodySmall line "Puedes corregir ingresos de meses anteriores…"; and, when correcting across months, "Esta corrección afecta los ingresos de …".
  6. Action (:375): error or missing message, then one full-width SelloButton in ButtonTone.Gain.
  7. AmountKeypad, compact.

Controls

  • X and Back: Back; asks before discarding something typed; ignored while busy or pending.
  • "Borrar" (correcting): Delete, no question; Deshacer comes from the correction notice.
  • "Gasto" on the switch: back to Anotar in place.
  • Day pill: OpenDatePicker. Paste: Paste.
  • "Otro ingreso": Several, to Varios ingresos with source, Otro's name, amount, note and date as line one.
  • A source chip: SourcePicked. "Otro"'s line: OpenComposer(SourceName). The note line: OpenComposer(Note).
  • Se repite's row: Repeat(…); the button: Submit, or Retry when the outcome is unknown.
  • The pad: digits, "000", backspace, long press to clear.

Screen: Ingresos de octubre (income history)

Layout

Drawn inside the shell (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/IncomeDestinations.kt:45): a back arrow, the title "Ingresos de octubre" (with the year when not the current one), the past-month ribbon over a past month, the dock "Anotar un ingreso" / "Anotar ingreso", and the correction notice bar. The content is a LazyColumn spaced by md (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/income/IncomeHistoryScreen.kt:72):

  1. Stale notice, when a refresh failed (:175).
  2. AnswerSlip "Entró en octubre" with the month's income, neutral ink, no stamp; under a TearLine a SplitBar (budgets solid, unassigned striped) and two LeaderLines "En presupuestos de categorías" and "Sin asignar" (red when negative), then the over-basis and carry notes (:307).
  3. "Plan del mes", a PlanSlip: "Anotado", "Esperado", a TotalLine "Planeado", "Sin asignar en el plan", the ExpectedChips and the footnote (:240).
  4. The suggestion, a SuggestionNote "Una sugerencia" (:196).
  5. "Lo que entró": a Slip with a titleMedium heading, one LeaderLine per income (source, then "date · note"), "Ver más ingresos", the TotalLine "Total" and a Text button "Anotar varios" with a receipt icon (:116).

  • IncomeDatePicker (IncomeFormScreen.kt:498) against MovementDatePicker (component/Movement.kt:146) and the expense form's DayPicker. Finding: C01.

  • Raw Text hints (IncomeFormScreen.kt:356, :363; IncomeHistoryScreen.kt:355, :367, :288) where the expense form uses FieldMessageText with the Hint tone. Finding: C15.
