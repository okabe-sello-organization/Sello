Feature

Recibo

What is left this month and where it is going, one category at a time, with the screens Recibo opens: category detail, every expense of a category, Pagos previstos and Ajustar presupuesto with its limit sheet.

Purpose

Recibo answers "how much is left this month" as one signed figure with a verdict stamp, then shows each category as a circle with what it has left. From it a person opens a category's detail and expenses, sees the payments the month reserves, and adjusts limits when the month is over budget. The code for all of these lives in app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/; the goal pill drawn inside Recibo belongs to the goals feature, the notice bars to the shell and recurrence.

Every figure comes from one monthly snapshot of the shared selected month (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/MonthSession.kt:33), so Recibo, category detail, Ajustar presupuesto and Pagos previstos always agree. A failed read never turns into zero: the last good figures stay with an older-figures notice.

Entry points

  • Recibo: the start destination (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:94) and the Recibo tab (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:84); composed at app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:76.
  • Category detail: a Recibo cell (ShellAction.OpenCategory, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:348) — beside Recibo when there is room for two panes, in its place otherwise; a ranking row on Resumen (ResumenCategoryRoute, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:55). Described on Category.
  • Todos los gastos (every expense of a category): "Ver todos" on category detail (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:106, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:93). Described on Category.
  • Pagos previstos: the reserved footnote on Recibo (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:109) and on Ajustar presupuesto (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:221).
  • Ajustar presupuesto: "Ajustar presupuesto ›" on Recibo's answer slip or on category detail in Revisar (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:116, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:104). The route carries the month it was opened on.
  • Limit sheet: a category row on Ajustar presupuesto (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/AdjustBudgetScreen.kt:84).

Screen: Recibo

Layout

Inside the shell with tabs, the month in the title, a settings action (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:300), the Anotar dock and a notice bar (a correction just made first, otherwise catch-up's, ReciboDestinations.kt:290). One scrolling column, slips spaced spacing.md (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/ReciboScreen.kt:98).

  1. Carry problem slip, only when a carry figure cannot be calculated: the app-shared CarryProblemSlip with "retry" and "set a limit" ways out (ReciboScreen.kt:128).
  2. Older-figures notice, only when stale: StaleSlip (ReciboScreen.kt:137).
  3. Answer slip: AnswerSlip with the label ("Restante del presupuesto", "Has gastado este mes" or "Falta para tus pagos"), a Stamp, and the signed amount (loss ink when Excedido). Its detail, in order (ReciboScreen.kt:187):
    • a finished month: "Mes cerrado · gastaste X de Y." (ReciboScreen.kt:405);
    • Revisar: the two facts "Quedan … y tus pagos previstos suman …." (ReciboScreen.kt:226);
    • the month in progress with a limit: a TearLine, then what can be spent per day as a MoneyText MoneyStyle.Card with "por día" and "N días, incluido hoy" (0 in loss ink when over) (ReciboScreen.kt:361);
    • while a category carries: MonthCarrySum — a strong LeaderLine "Límite del mes", the caption "X base + ↻ Y sobrante" (or "− ↻ … faltante") with an inline CarryBadge, a LeaderLine "Gastado" and "Cifras al …" (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/MonthCarrySum.kt:43);
    • while payments are reserved: a Footnote "Incluye X separado para N pagos previstos ›" (ReciboScreen.kt:243);
    • a month in progress that is Excedido or Revisar: the link "Ajustar presupuesto ›" (ReciboScreen.kt:265).
  4. Closed-month invitation, in the first week of a month whose previous month had expenses and was not handled: one dashed line with the Insights icon, "Ver cómo cerraste octubre", a chevron and a × (ReciboScreen.kt:300).
  5. Goal slot: whatever the goals feature draws (ReciboGoal), read apart so the answer never waits on it (ReciboScreen.kt:156).
  6. Categories slip (Slip, pinked = false): heading "Tus categorías" with a BandChip "+ Nueva categoría" (onPaper = true); a grid of CategoryCells, two columns or three when wide (categoryColumns); archived ones last under "Archivadas" in their own grid (ReciboScreen.kt:431). Each cell: a CategoryCircle filled to how much was used (∞ badge without a limit on purpose, grey when archived, ↻ badge while carrying), the name, the cell amount (loss ink when over, soft ink with "gastado"/"sin límite" under it when there is no finite limit) and, while carrying, the signed carry in gain or loss ink. Beside the detail pane, the open category's cell is tinted brandSoft (ReciboScreen.kt:474).

Controls

  • A cell: ReciboAction.OpenCategory; the shell remembers which category is open, so it survives rotation and process death. Long press (or the screen reader's custom action): a menu with "Editar categoría" (EditCategory → the category editor on the selected month).
  • "+ Nueva categoría" and "Crear categoría": CreateCategory → the new-category form.
  • "Incluye … separado …": OpenReserved → Pagos previstos.
  • "Ajustar presupuesto ›": AdjustBudget → Ajustar presupuesto for the selected month.
  • The invitation line: OpenInvitation(month) → picks that month and switches to Resumen; its ×: DismissInvitation(month). Both mark the month handled.
  • "Reintentar" (stale, error, carry problem): Retry, a new observation of the same month. The carry problem's other way out: SetMonthLimit(categoryId, month) → the category editor on that month.
  • Settings action in the top bar ("Abrir configuración") and the shell's month picker, tabs and dock belong to the shell.
  • System Back with the detail open in one pane closes it (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:385).

Category detail and Todos los gastos

A tap on a cell of the categories grid sends ShellAction.OpenCategory (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:348) and opens that category's detail: beside Recibo when there is room for two panes, in its place otherwise (the shell's frame for it is on Shell). The detail, its Pagos fijos section and Todos los gastos live in this feature's code but are described on the Category page: Category detail, Pagos fijos and Todos los gastos.

Screen: Pagos previstos

Layout

Its own screen inside the shell: title "Pagos previstos", back arrow, no tabs, no past-month ribbon, no Anotar dock (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:176). Always the month in progress, from the same snapshot as Recibo's footnote. A LazyColumn spaced spacing.md (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/UpcomingPayments.kt:170).

  1. StaleSlip when stale.
  2. AnswerSlip "Separado en octubre" with the total, no stamp; under it "2 pagos previstos, hasta el sáb 31 oct", a TearLine and one ScheduleRow per payment (category icon, "Categoría · día").
  3. A footnote paragraph in bodySmall inkSoft.
  4. A text button "Ver todos los pagos fijos".

Screen: Ajustar presupuesto

Layout

Its own screen inside the shell: title "Ajustar presupuesto", back arrow, no tabs, no ribbon, no dock (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:202). For the month it was opened on. Scrolling column spaced spacing.md; the limit sheet is drawn over it (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/AdjustBudgetScreen.kt:106).

  1. StaleSlip when stale.
  2. Month slip: AnswerSlip with Recibo's label, stamp and amount; the shortfall facts in Revisar; a TearLine; a LeaderLine "Límite de octubre" (with the caption "base X · de meses anteriores ±Y" while a category carries); a LeaderLine "Gastado"; and the reserved Footnote (AdjustBudgetScreen.kt:159).
  3. Categories slip (plain Slip): "Tus categorías", "Toca una para cambiar su límite de octubre.", one LimitRow per limited category (overspent first), then "Sin límite" and "Archivadas" groups headed in titleSmall inkSoft (AdjustBudgetScreen.kt:200). Each row: 36 dp CategoryCircle, name, caption ("gastaste X de Y", "gastado", "sin límite", "Archivada · desarchívala para cambiar su límite"), the amount left (loss ink when over) or spent, and a chevron.

Sheet: Limit of one category

Layout

A RepeatSheet over Ajustar presupuesto, titled "Límite de Café" (or "Límite base de …" while it carries) (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/AdjustBudgetScreen.kt:302).

  1. A row: 40 dp CategoryCircle, "Gastaste 90.000 de 50.000 en octubre" (or the carry/no-limit variant) in bodySmall inkSoft, and a close IconButton.
  2. The read failure (loss-ink line and "Reintentar") instead of everything below, or the older-figures line and "Reintentar".
  3. SegmentedSwitch "Solo octubre" / "Desde octubre" and the line explaining which months change.
  4. An amount box on paperAlt with shapes.control: "Límite en pesos" and "antes 50.000" over the typed amount (MoneyStyle.Total), or a "0" in rule colour when empty.
  5. A sideways-scrolling row of SelloChip shortcuts: "Lo gastado · X", "Cubre el mes · X", "Cubre tus pagos · X", "Base que cubre lo gastado · X".
  6. A FactLine: "Tu límite total de octubre pasaría a X. Te quedarían Y." and its variants.
  7. The error line (loss ink, or warn ink when the outcome is unknown) and, for a conflict or changed data, "Reintentar".
  8. A full-width filled SelloButton "Guardar solo octubre" / "Guardar desde octubre".
  9. A compact AmountKeypad.

  • Switch: LimitSheetAction.Scope. Keypad: Digit, TripleZero, Backspace, ClearAll. Chip: Chip(kind) types its amount.
