Feature

Settings

Configuración: the person's data card, what repeats, appearance, Recibo order and investment preferences on one scrolling screen.

Purpose

Lets a person change how Sello looks and orders things (theme, ink, category order and direction, the currency and order of new investments), see how many payments and incomes repeat and the current dollar rate, and reach backup, export and erase-everything. Every control is a real, saved setting.

Entry points

  • The gear icon in Recibo's top bar ("Abrir configuración", settings_open): app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:301, navigating with app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:101.
  • The destination itself, inside ShellScreen with a back arrow and the title "Configuración" (settings_title), no past-month ribbon, and the entry dock still shown: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SettingsDestinations.kt:24. System Back pops: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SettingsDestinations.kt:53.

From Configuración the person goes on to Pagos e ingresos fijos (SettingsDestinations.kt:40), Dólar (SettingsDestinations.kt:42), Respaldo (SettingsDestinations.kt:47) and Borrar datos (SettingsDestinations.kt:49).

Screen: Configuración

Layout

One vertically scrolling Column with spacing.md between blocks (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/SettingsScreen.kt:96). Top to bottom:

  1. A StatusSlip only when a preference read failed (Older, over the last good values) or a write failed (ActionFailed) (SettingsScreen.kt:108).
  2. Tus datos, a slot filled by navigation with Respaldo's TusDatosRoot (SettingsScreen.kt:115; described on the Backup page): backup card, Exportar row and "Borrar todos los datos".
  3. Lo que se repite: a Slip with a section title and one SettingRow "Pagos e ingresos fijos" showing the count (SettingsScreen.kt:183).
  4. Apariencia: a Slip with the "Tema" row and a SegmentedSwitch Sistema / Claro / Oscuro, then the "Tinta" row and the feature-local InkSwatches (SettingsScreen.kt:209).
  5. Recibo: a Slip with "Orden de las categorías" and "Dirección" rows, each opening an OptionSlip (SettingsScreen.kt:251).
  6. Inversiones: a Slip with "Moneda al crear" and "Orden de la lista" (each opening an OptionSlip) and "Dólar", which leads to the rate screen (SettingsScreen.kt:276).
  7. A centred bodySmall footer in inkSoft: "Sello · tus datos no salen de este teléfono" (SettingsScreen.kt:120).

Section titles, Tus datos' among them, are the design system's SectionTitle: titleMedium, bottom padding spacing.sm, heading semantics. The first SettingRow under each title passes divider = false.

Controls

  • Pagos e ingresos fijos row: opens the schedules list (onOpenSchedules, SettingsScreen.kt:202).
  • Tema switch: sends SettingsAction.ThemeChosen; the app's theme follows the stored preference through AppearanceViewModel (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/AppearanceViewModel.kt:15, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/AppearanceTheme.kt:10).
  • Tinta swatches (radio group): send SettingsAction.InkChosen (SettingsScreen.kt:238).
  • Orden de las categorías and Dirección rows: open an OptionSlip; a choice sends OrderChosen / DirectionChosen and closes it; Cancelar, Back or a tap outside closes it (SettingsScreen.kt:129, SettingsScreen.kt:141).
  • Moneda al crear and Orden de la lista rows: the same, with NewInvestmentCurrencyChosen / InvestmentOrderChosen (SettingsScreen.kt:155, SettingsScreen.kt:167).
  • Dólar row: opens the rate screen (onOpenRate, SettingsScreen.kt:318).
  • Reintentar on the StatusSlip: SettingsAction.Retry clears the write failure and re-reads (SettingsViewModel.kt:159).

Feature-local parts that look like shared ones

  • InkSwatches (SettingsComponents.kt:33) is a radio choice like SegmentedSwitch and OptionSlip, but drawn as colour circles. It re-declares DISABLED_ROW_ALPHA = 0.38f (SettingsComponents.kt:93) with the same name and value as SettingRow's private constant (design-system/src/main/java/com/software/sello/designsystem/component/Settings.kt:133), and hard-codes a 6.dp gap and a 2.5.dp ring. No other feature draws colour swatches. Finding: H69.
  • The schedule count and the rate say "unreadable" in a row caption, where a failed preference read shows an Older StatusSlip.

Findings here

Places in this feature on the findings page; shared parts it uses are listed there under their own files.

Gaps

  • InkSwatches is captured as part of settings-loaded, not on its own; its behaviour is tested in InkSwatchesTest.kt.
