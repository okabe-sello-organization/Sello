Feature

Settings

Configuración: the person's data card, what repeats, appearance, Recibo order and investment preferences on one scrolling screen.

Purpose

Lets a person change how Sello looks and orders things (theme, ink, category order and direction, the currency and order of new investments), see how many payments and incomes repeat and the current dollar rate, and reach backup, export and erase-everything. Every control is a real, saved setting.

Entry points

  • The gear icon in Recibo's top bar ("Abrir configuración", settings_open): app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:301, navigating with app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:101.
  • The destination itself, inside ShellScreen with a back arrow and the title "Configuración" (settings_title), no past-month ribbon, and the entry dock still shown: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SettingsDestinations.kt:25. System Back pops: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SettingsDestinations.kt:54.

From Configuración the person goes on to Pagos e ingresos fijos (SettingsDestinations.kt:41), Dólar (SettingsDestinations.kt:43), Respaldo (SettingsDestinations.kt:48) and Borrar datos (SettingsDestinations.kt:50).

Screen: Configuración

Layout

One vertically scrolling Column with spacing.md between blocks (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/SettingsScreen.kt:117). Top to bottom:

  1. A StatusSlip only when a preference read failed (Older, over the last good values) or a write failed (ActionFailed) (SettingsScreen.kt:125).
  2. Tus datos, a slot filled by navigation with Respaldo's TusDatosRoot (SettingsScreen.kt:140; described on the Backup page): backup card, Exportar row and "Borrar todos los datos".
  3. Lo que se repite: a Slip with a section title and one SettingRow "Pagos e ingresos fijos" showing the count (SettingsScreen.kt:209).
  4. Apariencia: a Slip with the "Tema" row and a SegmentedSwitch Sistema / Claro / Oscuro, then the "Tinta" row and the feature-local InkSwatches: Cobalto, Violeta, then Tuya (SettingsScreen.kt:235). Tuya is a rainbow with a + until it is chosen; chosen, it is its ink's colour with an edit badge, and a bodySmall note in inkSoft names the colour: "Rojo. Toca Tuya para cambiarla." (SettingsScreen.kt:284). Choosing another ink forgets Tuya's hue, so Tuya is a rainbow again.
  5. Recibo: a Slip with "Orden de las categorías" and "Dirección" rows, each opening an OptionSlip (SettingsScreen.kt:300).
  6. Inversiones: a Slip with "Moneda al crear" and "Orden de la lista" (each opening an OptionSlip) and "Dólar", which leads to the rate screen (SettingsScreen.kt:325).
  7. A centred bodySmall footer in inkSoft: "Sello · tus datos no salen de este teléfono" (SettingsScreen.kt:146).

Section titles, Tus datos' among them, are the design system's SectionTitle: titleMedium, bottom padding spacing.sm, heading semantics. The first SettingRow under each title passes divider = false.

Controls

  • Pagos e ingresos fijos row: opens the schedules list (onOpenSchedules, SettingsScreen.kt:227).
  • Tema switch: sends SettingsAction.ThemeChosen; the app's theme follows the stored preference through AppearanceViewModel (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/AppearanceViewModel.kt:15, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/settings/AppearanceTheme.kt:11).
  • Tinta swatches (radio group): Cobalto and Violeta send SettingsAction.InkChosen (SettingsScreen.kt:271); Tuya, set or not, opens Tu tinta with SettingsAction.Tuya.Open (SettingsScreen.kt:278).
  • Orden de las categorías and Dirección rows: open an OptionSlip; a choice sends OrderChosen / DirectionChosen and closes it; Cancelar, Back or a tap outside closes it (SettingsScreen.kt:154, SettingsScreen.kt:166).
  • Moneda al crear and Orden de la lista rows: the same, with NewInvestmentCurrencyChosen / InvestmentOrderChosen (SettingsScreen.kt:180, SettingsScreen.kt:192).
  • Dólar row: opens the rate screen (onOpenRate, SettingsScreen.kt:366).
  • Reintentar on the StatusSlip: SettingsAction.Retry clears the write failure and re-reads (SettingsViewModel.kt:179).

Sheet: Tu tinta

Layout

A SelloSheet titled "Tu tinta" over the whole of Configuración, top bar and dock included: TuyaSheetRoot is drawn after the screen in its destination and shares its view model (SettingsScreen.kt:86, SettingsDestinations.kt:56). Top to bottom (TuyaSheet.kt:75):

  1. Two previews side by side, Claro and Oscuro: a small Recibo (band with the month and a gear, the remaining figure with an "Al día" Stamp, and an Anotar bar) drawn inside its own SelloTheme(HueInk(hue)), so every colour is the generator's. Sample content; hidden from TalkBack (TuyaSheet.kt:167).
  2. The colour's name, titleLarge, centred (hueName).
  3. The hue slider: a 28 dp track drawn in the light band of 61 evenly spaced hues and a 34 dp thumb in the chosen one, ringed in paper and outline; 48 dp tall (TuyaSheet.kt:250).
  4. The note "El rojo y el verde también marcan excesos y metas." in bodySmall ink on red (0–49°) and green (122–195°) hues. Its line is always laid out, so the buttons never move.
  5. After a failed save, a FieldMessageText error.
  6. A ButtonPair: Cancelar (outlined) and Usar esta tinta (filled), or Reintentar after a failure.

Controls

  • Slider: a tap or drag picks the hue under the finger (0 at the left end, 359 at the right) with SettingsAction.Tuya.HueChanged. TalkBack reads "Tono de tu tinta" and the colour's name as its state, and moves it with setProgress, a step at a time; a step past an end stops there (TuyaSheet.kt:269).
  • Usar esta tinta / Reintentar: SettingsAction.Tuya.Use stores Tuya with its hue in one write.
  • Cancelar, back (TuyaSheet.kt:81) and the scrim: SettingsAction.Tuya.Cancel closes it and forgets the hue; the ink stays.

Feature-local parts that look like shared ones

  • InkSwatches (SettingsComponents.kt:51) is a radio choice like SegmentedSwitch and OptionSlip, but drawn as colour circles. Disabled, it fades by SelloTheme.alpha.disabled like SettingRow; it hard-codes a 6.dp gap and a 2.5.dp ring. No other feature draws colour swatches.
  • HueSlider (TuyaSheet.kt:250) is a slider of its own, not Material's: its track is drawn in the generator's colours and TalkBack reads the colour's name. It hard-codes a 28 dp track and a 34 dp thumb.
  • The schedule count and the rate say "unreadable" in a row caption, where a failed preference read shows an Older StatusSlip.
