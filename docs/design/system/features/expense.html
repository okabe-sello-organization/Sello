Feature

Expense

Record one expense with the pad, or correct or delete one already recorded.

Purpose

"Anotar" lets a person type an amount on the pad, pick a category, and optionally change the day, add a note or make it repeat, then record it with one button. The same screen, opened on a recorded expense, becomes "Editar gasto": it corrects or deletes that expense. It can hand what is typed to Anotar varios and switch to Anotar ingreso.

Screen: Anotar / Editar gasto

Layout

The whole form is an EntryLayout (ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:231): the amount, the button and the pad hold still and only the choices scroll; a wide or short window puts the button and pad beside the rest.

  1. Top bar (SelloScaffold) on the brand band: an X, the title "Anotar" or "Editar gasto", and when correcting a text button "Borrar" on the band.
  2. Header: a SegmentedSwitch "Gasto | Ingreso", only for a new expense opened from a place that offers income (:235).
  3. The amount slip, EntryAmountSlip: "Gasto en pesos", a FieldPill with the day, the chosen category's icon, the amount and a paste button (:379). The brand band runs down to its middle.
  4. MoreSlip "Otro gasto", tucked under the amount, when Anotar varios can be reached and the expense is new (:251).
  5. Choices, scrolling (:421): a stale notice if any; category SelloChips in ChipRows, the chosen one first (rememberSelectedFirst), ending with an AddChip "Nueva" for a new expense; the note's EntryLine; Se repite's row (new expense only); the past-month hint; the past-expense announcement.
  6. Action (:561): what is missing or went wrong as FieldMessageText, then one full-width SelloButton.
  7. AmountKeypad, compact (:277).

Controls

  • X and system Back: Back. With something typed it asks first (discard slip); while busy or pending nothing happens.
  • "Borrar" (correcting, once read): Delete. It does not ask; Deshacer is offered afterwards by the correction notice.
  • "Ingreso" on the switch: swaps to Anotar ingreso in place.
  • Date pill: OpenDatePicker. Paste button: Paste with the clipboard text untouched.
  • "Otro gasto": Several, which opens Anotar varios with what is typed as line one.
  • A category chip: CategoryPicked. "Nueva": the category editor; the new category comes back chosen (ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:126).
  • The note line ("Agregar nota" / the note, "Editar"): OpenNote.
  • Se repite's row: Repeat(…), opening the recurrence sheet.
  • The button: Submit, or Retry when the outcome is unknown.
  • The pad: Digit, TripleZero ("000"), Backspace; a long press on backspace is ClearAmount.

Dialog: discard the draft

A ConfirmSlip with destructive = false, so "Descartar" is in brand blue (ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:290). New: "¿Descartar este gasto?" / "Lo que escribiste no se guardará." / "Seguir anotando". Correcting: "¿Descartar los cambios?" / "El gasto queda como estaba." / "Seguir editando". "Descartar" sends DiscardConfirmed, the other button KeepEditing. No screenshot.

Dialog: date picker

Material DatePickerDialog with a SelloButton "Aceptar" (expense_entry_date_confirm) (ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:673). Days after today cannot be picked; while Se repite is on, only today or later. Whole days in UTC, no time or zone. Dismiss sends CloseDatePicker. No screenshot.

Sheet: note composer

NoteComposer docked above the keyboard (ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:626). Its context line reads "Gasto en Alimentación · 48.700" with the category icon (or a receipt icon), a counter "%1$d/%2$d" up to 60, the hint "Opcional", and "Listo" / "Cancelar". Over 60 characters it says "Máximo 60 caracteres. Tu texto sigue aquí." and cannot finish; a line break gives "La nota no puede tener saltos de línea." No screenshot.
