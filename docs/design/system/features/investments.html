Feature

Investments

Write down what is invested, what it is worth now and what moved in or out, in pesos or dollars, and see the total and the return.

Purpose

Inversiones lists every investment with its value and signed return and adds them up into one total (dollars converted at a rate the person types in). Each investment has a detail page with a chart of recorded values against what was put in, and its movements. Movements are aportes, values of the day, retiros and dividends, typed one at a time or several on one receipt. Sello never fetches a price or a rate. Every figure stays in its investment's own currency.

Screen: Inversiones

Layout

  1. Shell: the title "Inversiones" (never a month) and, once there is an investment, the BandChip "Nueva inversión" beside it (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/InvestmentDestinations.kt:86).
  2. The total as an AnswerSlip: "Tus inversiones valen", the figure, the stamp "Gana" or "Pierde" (none when even), then PerformanceLine (arrow, signed percentage, the gain in words), "En total, desde …", a TearLine, four LeaderLines (Aportaste, Retiraste, Dividendos y rentas, Ganancia in gain or loss ink), a quiet line with the dates of the values and the rate line (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/investments/InversionesScreen.kt:143).
  3. The list in a plain Slip: heading "Dónde está, de mayor a menor", a SplitBar with each part's percent written inside (gap 3dp, height 22dp), one InvestmentRow per investment whose circle takes the same ink as its part, and "Anotar varios" (…/InversionesScreen.kt:231).
  4. Dock: "Agregar inversión" while there are none, otherwise "Anotar movimiento"; both ink with the Add icon so they are never taken for Anotar (brand) or Metas (green) (…/InvestmentDestinations.kt:186).

Screen: Investment detail

Layout

  1. Shell: back arrow, the investment's name as title, the pencil (IconButton with EditNote, "Editar inversión") once loaded, and the ink dock "Anotar movimiento" (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/InvestmentDestinations.kt:131).

The answer: an AnswerSlip "Vale" with the figure and Gana / Pierde, PerformanceLine, "Valor anotado el … · desde …", a TearLine, the four LeaderLines and an outlined ink SelloButton "Anotar el valor de hoy" (Insights icon). The chart slip "Valor y lo que pusiste": a line above it with the picked value's day, "Vale …" and "Pusiste …"; then RecordedValuesChart (recorded values as dots joined by a line in brand, what was put in as a dashed step line, short amounts at the left, first, middle and last month underneath, a legend) (…/InvestmentDetailScreen.kt:192). Shown only with at least one recorded value.
  4. The movements slip "Movimientos": the newest four as MovementRows (all of them, with a MovementMonthLine per month, once asked), then a foot with "Varios movimientos" and "Ver los N" (…/InvestmentDetailScreen.kt:289).

Controls

  • ✕ and system Back: Back; asks before throwing away a change, closes otherwise.
  • Icon circle: opens the icon sheet; a pick sends IconPicked.
  • Pesos | Dólares: CurrencyPicked (new only).

  • ⋮ "Borrar inversión": AskToDelete, then the confirm's "Borrar": Delete; deleting returns to Inversiones.
