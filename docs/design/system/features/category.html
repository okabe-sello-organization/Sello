Feature

Category

One category in a month: what is left of its limit, its expenses and fixed payments, and the form that creates, edits, archives or deletes it.

Purpose

A person looks at one category's month (remaining, spent, limit, carry, its latest expenses, its fixed payments and a few numbers about how it is going), opens every expense of it, and creates or changes the category itself: its name, icon, monthly limit and whether it carries what is left over. From the same form they archive it, unarchive it or delete it, with the deletion moving or deleting what it holds after saying exactly what that is.

The detail and the expense list live in feature/recibo; the form, carry and deletion live in feature/category. This page covers both, as one feature.

Screen: Category detail

Layout

A ShellScreen detail (ShellDetail) whose title is the category name and whose action is the edit icon; its dock is Anotar in this category, or disabled "Archivada" for an archived one (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ShellScreen.kt:68). The content scrolls (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/CategoryDetailScreen.kt:106), top to bottom, spaced by spacing.md:

  1. A carry problem slip, when a carry figure cannot be calculated (CarryProblemSlip), and the older-figures StatusSlip Older after a failed refresh.
  2. The header Slip (:230): only beside Recibo, the name in headlineSmall with the edit icon; the headline ("Te quedan aquí", "Te pasaste aquí por", "Te sobraron aquí", "Has gastado aquí") in bodyLarge inkSoft with the verdict Stamp (or a Neutral "Archivada" stamp); a 56 dp CategoryCircle beside the big FittedMoneyText (44 sp start, loss ink when over); the carry "why" note; Revisar's shortfall note and "Ajustar presupuesto" link when payments are reserved; then either the CarryBreakdownSection under a TearLine or "Gastado …" and "de …" in labelMedium; under a second tear line "Para N días", "Al día" and "Frente a …" (plain ink when higher or equal, gain when lower); the zero-allowance deficit line; the reserved footnote that scrolls to Pagos fijos.
  3. For a month that is over: "… ya terminó. Su límite aún se puede corregir en Editar categoría…" and, once, the past-limit SelloNotice (:172).
  4. Pagos fijos (below).
  5. The latest expenses (:476): a plain slip with "Últimos gastos" (titleMedium) and a Text-kind "Ver todos"/"Ver los N"; the always-on hint; a delete problem, if any; up to four ActionRows of ExpenseStubLine separated by HorizontalDivider(paperAlt); "Anotar varios" as a Text button with the receipt icon.
  6. The insight slips (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recibo/CategoryInsightSections.kt:46): "En números" (count, average, largest, costliest day), "Cada semana gastas" with a ColumnsChart of weeks and LeaderLines, "Un mes normal aquí" with the monthly chart, its dotted limit line and legend.

Controls

  • Edit icon (title bar or beside the name): opens Editar categoría for the month shown.
  • Tap an expense: opens its edit. Its row actions "Editar" and "Borrar", swipe away or long-press: CategoryDetailAction.Delete.
  • "Comprobar si se borró": CategoryDetailAction.CheckDelete, the same operation asked again.
  • "Ver todos" / "Ver los N": Todos los gastos. "Anotar varios": bulk entry with this category. "Anotar aquí" (empty list) and the dock: Anotar in this category.
  • Reserved footnote: scrolls Pagos fijos into view. "Ajustar presupuesto": the budget adjuster.
  • Every retry: CategoryDetailAction.Retry.

Screen: Nueva categoría / Editar categoría

Layout

One form for both (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/category/CategoryEditorScreen.kt:140), drawn by CategoryEditorRoot with CategoryEditorViewModel (new) or CategoryManagementRoot with CategoryManagementViewModel (existing).

  1. SelloScaffold: title "Nueva categoría" or "Editar categoría", a × "Cerrar" icon button like every full-screen form, and for an existing category that was read a MoreMenu with "Archivar categoría" and "Borrar categoría" (loss), or only "Borrar categoría" when archived (:148).
  2. EntryLayout, as Anotar: header, amount, choices, action, keypad (:202).
  3. Header: with forEntry, the hint "Para anotar un gasto primero necesitas una categoría…"; then a plain slip with IconPickerCircle ("Elegir ícono" or "Cambiar") beside FormField "Nombre" with its counter "N/24" (:379). The name takes focus and the keyboard once. An archived category adds the Archivada slip: label and Neutral stamp "Archivada", "No acepta gastos nuevos; su historia sigue aquí.", Outlined "Desarchivar" (:268).
  4. Amount: EntryAmountSlip "Límite mensual" with a SwitchRow "Sin límite" in its corner (feature-local switch, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/category/CategoryComponents.kt:40); suggestions as SelloChips in a ChipFlow; the month's total line (info icon, "Tu límite total de … pasaría a" and the amount in W700); the scope sentence in bodySmall inkSoft; and the carry switch under a tear line (:475). With Sin límite on, the amount and chips stay but are greyed to 0.38. An archived category shows a locked slip instead: "Está archivada: no tiene límite de aquí en adelante. Desarchívala para ponerle uno." (:477).
  5. Carry switch (only on the month in progress): "Pasar lo que sobre al mes siguiente" with, under it, "Si te pasas, se descuenta del mes siguiente. Empieza en 0 este mes." or "Desde … · trae … · este mes …". The switch is part of the draft (SELLO-188): it shows what Guardar will save and, while that differs from storage, says so instead: "Se activa al guardar: empieza en 0 en …", "Se apaga al guardar." or "Se apaga al guardar y olvida … de meses anteriores."; then the last refusal (loss: something to fix, as under the form) (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/category/CategoryCarry.kt:123).
  6. Choices: what went wrong saving (FieldMessageText: error, or warning for an unknown outcome), the month-ended sentence, and "Aún no cambiaste nada." while an existing category is unchanged (:542).
  7. Action: "Escribe un nombre." hint, then a full-width SelloButton: "Crear categoría" (Add icon) or "Guardar cambios"; "Comprobar si se guardó" after an unknown outcome; after a conflict "Conservar mis cambios" (filled, keeps what was typed) over "Volver a cargar" (outlined, drops it) (:571).
  8. Keypad: compact AmountKeypad, disabled under Sin límite, absent when the limit is locked (:228).

Flow: Borrar categoría

Layout

Each step is a slip or sheet over the editor (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/category/CategoryRemovalSlips.kt:80), driven by CategoryRemoval (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/category/CategoryRemoval.kt:71):

  1. Counting: RemovalDialog (the shared DialogSlip with one button or none) "¿Borrar …?" with a Material spinner and "Contando lo que tiene …" (:93); cannot be dismissed.
  2. Empty: ConfirmSlip "No tiene gastos ni pagos fijos. Se borran la categoría y su límite. Esto no se puede deshacer.", red "Borrar" (:107).
  3. Choose: RemovalDialog with the counts in bold, two feature-local ChoiceRows (icon circle, title, note, chevron): "Moverlos a otra categoría" and red "Borrar todo"; then the link "¿Solo quieres dejar de usarla? Archívala: no se borra nada." (not for an archived category); Outlined "Cancelar" under a tear line (:188).
  4. Pick: SelloSheet "Mover los gastos de … a" with a caption, then feature-local TargetRows (icon circle, name, what is left this month) and "Las archivadas no aparecen: no aceptan gastos." (:251).
  5. Move: ConfirmSlip "¿Mover a … y borrar …?" with what moves, that limits and carry go, and ChangeRows for the target's spending and both limits; red "Mover y borrar" (:124).
  6. All: ConfirmSlip "¿Borrar … y todo lo suyo?" with "Se borran para siempre:" and ChangeRows (expenses with their months and total, Pagos fijos, "Sus límites y su arrastre"), "Los meses pasados mostrarán menos gasto. Esto no se puede deshacer.", a FormField "Escribe BORRAR para confirmar"; red "Borrar todo" stays off until BORRAR is typed (:136, :397).
  7. Correction: ConfirmSlip with the target's later months that change (CarryCorrectionContent) (:146).
  8. Checking: RemovalDialog "Comprobando…", "Sello no supo si terminó de borrar … y lo está revisando. No se borrará dos veces.", Outlined "Comprobar de nuevo" after a failed check (:155).
  9. Problem: RemovalDialog titled with what happened, holding an ActionFailed StatusSlip: "No se borró ni se movió nada." and why, with new figures in bold, with its one way forward ("Revisar", "Elegir otra" or "Reintentar"), and Outlined "Cancelar" under the tear line. While the way forward runs, Cancelar, Back and a tap outside do nothing (:438).
