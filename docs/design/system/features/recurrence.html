Feature

Recurrence

Payments and incomes that repeat: the month's list, each one's editor, how it repeats, and what Sello recorded by itself.

Purpose

A person sets an expense or income to repeat ("Se repite"), sees every fixed payment or income with this month's plan (how much went through, how much is still to come), changes, pauses, resumes or deletes one from tomorrow on, and reviews what Sello recorded by itself when it caught up.

Code: feature/recurrence (list, editor, catch-up) and presentation/recurrence (the words and rules of Se repite's row, sheet and the "Solo este / Este y los siguientes" question, which Anotar and the income form also use; the parts themselves are the design system's component/RepeatParts.kt).

Entry points

  • Pagos e ingresos fijos (SchedulesRoute, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:32):
    • "Ver todos los pagos fijos" on a category's Pagos fijos (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:115) and on Pagos previstos (:197).
    • Resumen (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:57, :102).
    • Configuración's fixed-payments row (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SettingsDestinations.kt:40).
  • Pago fijo / Ingreso fijo (ScheduleRoute(id), app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:97): a row of the list (:51), of a category's Pagos fijos (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:113), of Pagos previstos (:194) or of Resumen (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ResumenDestinations.kt:99).
  • New fixed payment or income: the list's "Nuevo pago fijo" / "Nuevo ingreso fijo" opens Anotar or the income form with Se repite already on (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:53).
  • Se repite: the row under the note in Anotar, the income form and the schedule editor (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/expense/ExpenseEntryScreen.kt:472, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/income/IncomeFormScreen.kt:340, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recurrence/ScheduleEditorScreen.kt:312).
  • Catch-up notice: on Recibo above Anotar, after Sello recorded fixed payments or incomes (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:293); its "Ver" opens Lo que Sello anotó (CatchUpRoute, :108; destination app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:63).

Screen: Pagos e ingresos fijos

Layout

A ShellScreen with the title only, "Pagos e ingresos fijos" (no month to pick), a back arrow, no entry dock, and the correction notice bar when there is one (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/RecurrenceDestinations.kt:35). A lazy list spaced by spacing.md (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/recurrence/SchedulesScreen.kt:89):

  1. SegmentedSwitch "Pagos" / "Ingresos".
  2. The older-figures StatusSlip Older, when the last refresh failed.
  3. The month's receipt: a Slip with compact padding (16/15/16/12 dp) (:159): "Tus pagos fijos de …" (bodyMedium W700 inkSoft), the total in displaySmall (gain ink for incomes), a SplitBar of what went through, then two dotted parts "Ya se anotaron" / "Ya entraron" and "Faltan"; a TearLine; and a StubButton "+ Nuevo pago fijo" or "+ Nuevo ingreso fijo".
  4. The list slip (:334): uppercase group headings in labelMedium W800 inkSoft ("FALTAN EN … · N", "YA SE ANOTARON · N", "MÁS ADELANTE · N", "EN PAUSA · N", "TERMINADOS · N"), each followed by its ScheduleRows: the category's icon (AccountBalance for an income), the note or category or source as the name, the category, rule and state as caption, the amount, and a date pill ("Mañana, …", the day, or "próximo …"). Recorded ones show a check; paused and ended ones are dimmed.
  5. The footnote "Se anotan solos, cada uno en su fecha, cuando abres Sello." in bodySmall inkSoft.

Layout

  7. Over the form: the NoteComposer for the note or Otro's name; Material DatePickerDialog for the start (tomorrow or later) with a SelloButton confirm (:465); Se repite's sheet; the delete and discard ConfirmSlips.
