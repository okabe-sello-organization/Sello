Feature

Goals (Metas)

Save towards named goals: create them, put money in or take it out, earn four stamps on the way and mark a goal as achieved.

Purpose

A person names something they want ("Viaje a la costa"), sets how much it needs and, optionally, the month they want it by. Each aporte (contribution) or retiro (withdrawal) moves the goal's saved amount; crossing 25 %, 50 % and 75 % earns a stamp, and at 100 % the person marks it lograda (achieved) to earn the last stamp, the Meta, with a full-screen celebration. Up to five starred goals show on Recibo as a pill that is also a progress bar.

The code lives in app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/goals/; the routes and the band, dock and top-bar actions are in app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/GoalDestinations.kt.

Entry points

  • Metas tab in the bottom navigation, with the flag icon: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/SelloApp.kt:70, routed to MetasRoute at SelloApp.kt:82. Back on Metas returns to the Recibo tab: app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/GoalDestinations.kt:133.
  • Recibo's goal pill: the main part opens the goal (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/navigation/ReciboDestinations.kt:88), the round + opens Aportar with that goal chosen (ReciboDestinations.kt:91). The pill itself is drawn here, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/goals/ReciboGoal.kt:41, and placed by Recibo at ReciboDestinations.kt:369.
  • From Metas: a goal card or completed row opens the goal (GoalDestinations.kt:252); "Nueva meta" on the band and the empty dock "Crear una meta" open the editor (GoalDestinations.kt:254); the dock "Aportar a una meta" opens Aportar with no goal chosen (GoalDestinations.kt:256); "Anotar varios" opens the receipt with no goal chosen (GoalDestinations.kt:258).
  • From a goal: the pencil opens Editar meta (GoalDestinations.kt:277); the dock "Aportar a esta meta" (GoalDestinations.kt:279); "Retirar dinero" (GoalDestinations.kt:282); a movement row opens its correction (GoalDestinations.kt:287); "Aportar varios" (GoalDestinations.kt:291).
  • From Aportar / Retirar: the "Otro movimiento" slip replaces the form with Aportar varios, carrying what was typed as its first line (GoalDestinations.kt:321, GoalDestinations.kt:344).
  • Celebration: "Ver mi meta" on the Lograda moment from Metas opens that goal (GoalDestinations.kt:127).

Screen: Metas

Layout

  1. Band from ShellScreen titled "Metas" (never a month), with the BandChip "Nueva meta" beside the title once there is any goal (GoalDestinations.kt:90).
  2. A LazyColumn with SelloSpacing.lg between items (app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/goals/MetasScreen.kt:79).
  3. The total as an AnswerSlip: "Guardado en metas", the saved amount, no stamp, StampInk.Good, and under it a green "+ 2.160.000 en octubre" line when anything came in this month (MetasScreen.kt:226).
  4. One GoalCard per active goal, highest completion first (MetasScreen.kt:131); a reached goal carries a full-width green "Marcar como lograda" SelloButton under its card (MetasScreen.kt:148).
  5. "Anotar varios", a text SelloButton with the receipt icon, after the active goals and only while there is one (MetasScreen.kt:162).
  6. One CompletedGoalRow per completed goal; its "LOGRADA" Stamp comes down once per visit as the row first appears, without a haptic (MetasScreen.kt:175).
  7. Dock: green with the flag (goalDock, GoalDestinations.kt:226) — "Crear una meta" with no goals, "Aportar a una meta" otherwise; none while loading or failed.

Screen: Goal detail

Layout

  1. Band titled with the goal's name (fallback "Meta"), a back arrow, no tabs. On an active goal the band also holds the star pill (GoalPin → GoalPinPill, app/src/main/java/com/software/sello/feature/goals/GoalDetailScreen.kt:513). Top bar: the pencil IconButton "Editar meta" on an active goal (GoalDestinations.kt:180); on a completed goal a MoreMenu with "Borrar meta" in loss ink (GoalDestinations.kt:185).
  2. A scrolling column, SelloSpacing.lg apart (GoalDetailScreen.kt:89).
  3. Save problem slip (GoalSaveProblem) and stale notice when they apply.
  4. Hero, a pinked Slip (GoalDetailScreen.kt:201): "Ya guardaste" with the percentage in gain, the saved amount in FittedMoneyText (start 40), "de 3.000.000" and "faltan …", a 9 dp green bar, a TearLine, "Sellos" with "2 de 4", the MilestoneRow (lands earned stamps and relights returned ones in place), one FactLine per kept stamp, the next-stamp line, and "Marcar como lograda" (filled, Gain) when the goal is at 100 %.
  5. Facts, unpinked: LeaderLines "Meta", "Fecha objetivo", "Desde" (GoalDetailScreen.kt:325). Not shown on a completed goal.
  6. Aportes por mes: a ColumnsChart 120 dp tall, the current month in brand (GoalDetailScreen.kt:340). Hidden when there are no months.
  7. Movimientos: heading with "Retirar dinero" (text button) on an active goal; newest four MovementRows (aporte: + icon, gain amount; retiro: − icon (SelloIcon.Remove), ink amount; a note becomes the title; an earned stamp replaces the icon and adds "Ganaste el sello del …"; "saldo" under each amount); the full list adds MovementMonthLines with each month's net. Foot: "Aportar varios" and "Ver los N" (GoalDetailScreen.kt:375).
  8. Dock "Aportar a esta meta" (green, flag) on an active goal only (GoalDestinations.kt:199).
