Frame and navigation

SelloScaffold component/SelloScaffold.kt:77

Purpose
The frame of a screen: top bar, content on the desk, and the app's navigation and entry action arranged for the window it is given. In an upright phone or a medium window the dock and tab bar sit under the content, never over it. In a short or expanded window a rail at the leading edge carries the navigation and the dock's "+", and secondaryPane, if given, sits beside content; each pane scrolls on its own. Both bottom controls step aside for the system keyboard. A notice is drawn over the bottom of the content, above the dock, and moves nothing. A topBar takes the place of the title row (title, subtitle, titleContent, navigationIcon and actions then go unused). A ribbon, if given, sits on the band under the top bar or the title row; the band grows with it, so the content still starts on the band. A band colour puts the title row on a band of that colour: an entry form passes its kind's, so it is told apart before anything is typed. The scaffold holds no state: selection, the month and what a pane shows are the app's. @param blotterScroll how far, in pixels, the content has scrolled. The band shrinks by that much; the top bar keeps the band colour until the blotter is gone. @param windowInsets the system areas to keep clear. The paddings passed to content and secondaryPane already include what they must avoid.
Variants
blotter: Blotter.None 0 dp, Short 100 dp, Tall 132 dp of band under the status bar. Optional band colour (entry forms), ribbon, topBar, notice, secondaryPane, navigation, dock. Layout follows the window: tab bar and dock, or rail and two panes.
