Notes

  • Only expenses and incomes. Goal and investment movements stay in Metas and Inversiones (ADR 0011: independent tools).
  • Gastos / Ingresos chips filter both the day list and the search. The day total counts expenses only; incomes are never netted into it.
  • Every news state uses StatusSlip with its kind (E32): Empty for an empty month and for nothing found, LoadFailed, Older, and ActionFailed for a failed page.
  • The magnifier and share shapes are Material Symbols drawn here for the mock; the ticket adds the icon to the design system.
