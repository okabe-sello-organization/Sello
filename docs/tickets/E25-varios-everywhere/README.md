# SELLO-E25 — Varios from every entry sheet

- **Status:** Done
- **Increment:** Post-MVP
- **Goal:** The person reaches a bulk receipt from any entry sheet, and a receipt holds one kind of entry with nothing to switch.
- **Exit:** Aportar/Retirar and Anotar movimiento offer the Varios pill as Anotar does, and the expense and income receipt has no Gasto/Ingreso switch.

## Decisions

**Owner direction (2026-10-31):** bulk entry is for a single kind of entry, to cut the
steps it takes to bring the app up to date.

- Goals and investments get the same way in as expenses and incomes: the Varios pill on
  their entry sheets, with no new design; the [Anotar varios](../../design/v2/varios.html)
  pill and receipts are reused as they are.
- The expense and income receipt loses its Gasto/Ingreso switch: where it opens from sets
  its kind for good. To change kind, the person closes it and opens the other one. This
  replaces the switch E23 approved on 2026-10-30.
