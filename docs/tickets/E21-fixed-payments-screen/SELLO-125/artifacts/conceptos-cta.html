1 · El talón del recibo (main CTA)

The month's receipt ends in a tear-off stub, + Nuevo pago fijo, like the stub you liked on Resumen. The action belongs to the total it adds to; it scrolls with the receipt, which stays first on the screen.

2 · El mes en un calendario (main CTA)

The summary becomes February's calendar: days with a payment are marked (done in grey, still due in blue). Tap any day to add a payment on that day, which is how people think of them (“el 5 de cada mes”); the button under it does the same. Bigger change: a new calendar part.

3 · Ideas para agregar (main CTA)

Under the summary, one row of the usual ones (+ Arriendo, + Servicios, + Internet…): one tap opens the entry already named, with Se repite on. Empty, it becomes a grid of category circles asking “¿Qué pagas cada mes?”, the strongest empty state of the set.
