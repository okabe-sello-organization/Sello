Notes

  • Archived categories without spending that month are left out; an archived one with spending is shown, as Recibo does.
  • The figures come from the same snapshot as Recibo, so the image never shows numbers Recibo doesn't. Older (stale) figures can't be shared: the button waits for a fresh read.
  • The PNG is written to cache/exports/ (the only shared folder), replaces the previous export, and is deleted at the next start, like CSV exports today.
