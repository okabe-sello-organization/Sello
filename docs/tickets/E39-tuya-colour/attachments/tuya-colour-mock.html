Tuya con cualquier color

E39, approved by the owner on 2026-11-12. The person picks any colour; the light theme shows it exactly and Sello makes the dark theme from it. Drag the square or the bar, type a code, or tap Antes.

1 · Tu tinta

From top to bottom

  • Claro / Oscuro previews: the band with white text, a chip in the soft tint, Anotar, Restante with AL DÍA, a category over its limit (EXCEDIDO, “Te pasaste por”) and a goal band.
  • Name and code: the code is a field; tapping it lets the person type an exact one.
  • Colour square and colour bar.
  • Antes / Ahora: the ink in use when the sheet opened, and the one being made, each with white “Aa”. Tapping Antes goes back to it.
  • One line with either the hint or, near the excess red or the goal green, the note in a soft red box. It never changes height.
  • Cancelar / Usar esta tinta: nothing changes until Usar esta tinta.

Exact or made by Sello

  • Exact: light band and buttons; their text is always white.
  • Made from it: dark band (a little deeper, white text) and dark buttons (lighter, dark text); desk, paper, chips and text tints in the colour's hue. A grey gives a grey app.
  • Never change: gain, loss, warning, goal band and investment colours.
  • Nothing is blocked and there is no contrast warning.
