Notes

  • Gastos hormiga counts expenses whose amount is at most the line, in every category, fixed payments included (a 15.000 subscription is small too). The percentage is of the same days' spending, rounded half up to a whole number.
  • Lo que más se repite groups by the note as search folds it (accents, capitals and spaces at the ends ignored). Expenses without a note are not a group.
  • Tu año: 'cumplido' and 'excedido' are each month's own verdict from its history (ADR 0012 BG1); a month without a limit shows no mark. The comparison exists only when the earlier year has data for those days.
  • Records come only from complete months; Mes más tranquilo is the cheapest complete month with expenses.
  • Loading, failed read and older figures use StatusSlip; Tu año keeps its last good figures marked as older, like Resumen.
