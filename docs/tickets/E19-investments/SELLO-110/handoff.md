# SELLO-110 — Record investment movements and values

Inversiones is in the tab bar: Nueva / Editar inversión (`InvestmentEditorScreen`, rate asked first for dollars), the investment's detail with its values chart and movements (`InvestmentDetailScreen`), and Anotar movimiento (`InvestmentMovementScreen`) with corrections and Deshacer through `ExpenseCorrections`.
Every amount stays in its currency; dollars use the pad's comma; a recorded value may be 0.
Open: pasting a dollar amount is not offered; switching between a peso and a dollar investment clears the typed amount.
