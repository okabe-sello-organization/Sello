# SELLO-100 — Manage recurring payments and incomes

Se repite in both entry forms makes a schedule (`RepeatChoice`, `RepeatDraft`, `RepeatParts`); Pagos e ingresos fijos (`feature/recurrence`) lists them from Configuración and Resumen with the month as a plan (`ScheduleListPolicy`).
The editor changes, pauses, resumes and deletes one; saving new terms on a recorded entry asks Solo este / Este y los siguientes, schedule first.
Design system: `DayGrid`, `IntervalStepper`, `RepeatSheet`, `ScheduleRow`, `ChoiceSlip`, `SelloNotice(icon)` and four icons.
